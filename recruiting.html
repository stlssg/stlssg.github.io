PhD

English

One PhD position is offered each year, and applications are now open for the 2027 intake. The successful candidate will be co-supervised by professors at CityUHK’s main campus and will spend up to one year at the Hong Kong campus. The position is fully funded, and the degree will be awarded by City University of Hong Kong (main campus).

I am looking for highly self-motivated and hardworking students. Candidates interested in research topics including, but not limited to, computer networks, applied AI, network security, and communication systems are welcome to contact me.

Please use the following email subject: [PhD Position] – Your Name – Current Affiliation. Attach a brief self-introduction, your CV (in English or Chinese), proof of English proficiency (certificate or test score), and transcripts for both your bachelor’s and master’s studies.
